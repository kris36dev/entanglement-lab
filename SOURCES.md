## Original experiment choices

The 100 seeded complex input states, 21 visibility levels, 4,096 shots per setting, CSV schemas, and statistical reporting are choices made for this project. The noise sweep uses the explicitly stated Bell-state/global-mixture model; it is not copied hardware data.
